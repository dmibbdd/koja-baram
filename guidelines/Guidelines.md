# KAJA BERAM — Modern Field Guide

## Aesthetic Stance

**Modern Field Guide** — a cartographic, data-forward travel platform. The visual language draws from expedition atlases and survey maps: precise, informative, durable. Documentary photography framed within modules. Coordinate readouts in monospace. Topographic contour lines as structural texture.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sand | `#E6DDCB` | Page background |
| Forest Green | `#23412F` | Primary actions, headings, nav |
| Navy | `#1D2E44` | Secondary surfaces, data panels |
| Charcoal | `#3A3A38` | Body text |
| Warm White | `#F5F1E9` | Card surfaces |
| Moss | `#4A6741` | Hover states, secondary green |
| Tan | `#C8B99A` | Borders, dividers |
| Off-black | `#1A1A18` | Display headings |

## Typography

- **Headings**: Arvo (slab serif, Google Fonts) — restrained, durable
- **Body**: Inter (humanist sans) — readable, neutral
- **Utility / Coordinates / Labels**: Space Mono — cartographic precision

## Shape & Spacing

- Border radius: **4px** (sharp, map-module feel)
- Grid: 12-col desktop, 4-col mobile
- Card padding: 20px
- Section gaps: 64–96px

## Photography

Use Unsplash documentary-style imagery. Frame within containers with slight desaturation (filter: saturate(0.9)). Never full-bleed without a container boundary. Always paired with coordinate/metadata overlays.

## Signature Elements

- Topographic contour lines (SVG) behind hero and section headers
- Monospaced coordinate labels: `48.8566° N, 2.3522° E`
- Durable rectangular modules with thin #C8B99A borders
- Forest green CTAs with sand text
- Data density balanced with generous whitespace

## Never

- Nostalgic badges, crests, stamps
- Distressed or textured backgrounds
- Leather or paper material effects
- Full-bleed aspirational photography
- Serif display headlines (use slab serif, not decorative serif)
